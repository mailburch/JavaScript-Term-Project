------------------------------
    GAME DESCRIPTIONS
------------------------------
1)  Blackjack - A classic casino game where the player competes against the dealer to get a hand as close to 21 as possible without going over. Players can hit, stand, or double down to maximize their chances of winning.

2)  Mix & Match Puzzle - A tile-swapping puzzle game where players click on two tiles to swap them. The goal is to rearrange the tiles to form the original image.

3)  Memory Card Game - A concentration-based game where players flip over two cards at a time to find matching pairs. If the cards match, they remain face up. The game continues until all pairs are found.
